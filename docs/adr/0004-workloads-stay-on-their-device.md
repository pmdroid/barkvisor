# Workloads, disks, and Library copies stay on the Device that owns them

A Workload runs on exactly one Device. Its disks and its Library copies live on that Device. Pairing does not move guests, share disks, or replicate Images.

If a Device is wiped, it is a new Device. Re-pairing does not restore the Workloads that were on the old one. Other Devices are unaffected.
